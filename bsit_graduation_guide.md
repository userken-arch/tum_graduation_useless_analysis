### Stage 5: ⭐ Your Big Moment — BSIT Presentation (`[02:52:57]` – `[02:56:45]`)

Watch and listen closely at `[02:52:57]`. The announcer calls the Deans to present Bachelor's degrees:
> *"Mr. Chancellor sir, I now call upon the Director Institute of Computing and Informatics... to present the graduates for the conferment of Bachelor's degree of the Technical University of Mombasa."* (`[02:52:57]`)

#### 1. The Call to Stand:
At `[02:53:42]`, the Director of the Institute of Computing and Informatics steps to the microphone:
> *"Mr. Chancellor sir, I have the honor to present the following graduates who during the academic year satisfied the institute board of examiners and the senate for the conferment of bachelor's degree in the Institute of Computing and Informatics."*

* **ACTION:** Stand up immediately at your seat as **Bachelor of Science in Information Technology** and your honors classification are announced.
* Remain standing while the candidates are presented to the Chancellor.

#### 2. The Cue to Sit Down:
At `[02:56:45]`, the Director concludes:
> *"Graduates, please take your seats until the conferment of their respective bachelor's degree."*
* **ACTION:** Sit back down. (Degrees from the remaining five schools—Applied Sciences, Business, Engineering, Humanities, and Maritime—are now read).

### Stage 6: ⭐ Solemn Admission & The Tassel Flip (`[03:38:04]` – `[03:40:59]`)

Once all schools are read and the Medical Laboratory graduates conclude their professional oath (`[03:35:16] - [03:38:03]`):

#### 1. All Bachelor's Graduates Rise:
At `[03:38:04]`, the announcer calls:
> *"Will all the bachelor's degree graduates rise up for the conferment of the respective bachelor's degree by the chancellor."*
* **ACTION:** Stand up together with all bachelor's candidates across the entire stadium.

#### 2. Chancellor Pronounces the Statutory Formula:
At `[03:38:46]`, the Acting Chancellor delivers the statutory admission:
> *"Acting on the powers conferred to me... and having satisfied the university senate in all subjects for which you are enrolled, trained and examined, I admit the persons presented to me to the respective degrees of the Technical University of Mombasa and by my authority and that of the senate give power to read and to do all that appertains to this degree."*

#### 3. THE TASSEL FLIP & CELEBRATION (`[03:39:08]`):
* **ACTION:** Move your tassel from the **RIGHT side to the LEFT side** of your mortarboard.
* The arena erupts into applause and cheers (`[03:39:19]`). You are now officially a Bachelor of Science graduate!

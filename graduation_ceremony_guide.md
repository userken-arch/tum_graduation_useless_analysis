## 1. Universal Morning Checklist

- [ ] **Arrival Window:** Reach campus between **07:00 AM – 07:30 AM** before security gates congest.
- [ ] **Attire Under Gown:** Wear lightweight, breathable formal clothing (coastal heat and humidity inside tents can be intense).
- [ ] **The Tassel Rule:** Wear your mortarboard (cap) flat. Your tassel hangs on the **RIGHT side** of your face. It is only shifted to the **LEFT side** after the Chancellor officially admits you to your degree.
- [ ] **Seating by School:** Report directly to your faculty's designated seating pavilion. Stay in your department's section so you stand at the proper cue.
- [ ] **Essentials:** Cold water bottle, power bank, pocket mints/tissues.
- [ ] **Meeting Landmark:** Cellular data networks get heavily congested with thousands of attendees. Agree with family on a specific physical meeting landmark (e.g., *Campus Administration Gate* or *Library Steps*) beforehand.

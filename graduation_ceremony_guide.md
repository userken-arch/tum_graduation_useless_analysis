# TUM Graduation Ceremony: Universal Graduate Survival Guide

**Institution:** Technical University of Mombasa (TUM)  
**Ceremony:** 12th Graduation Ceremony (Class of 2025 — 3,281 Candidates)  
**Applicable To:** All Graduands (Certificates, Diplomas, Bachelor's, Master's, PhDs across all Schools)  
**Source Dataset:** [12th_graduation_ceremony_transcript.txt](12th_graduation_ceremony_transcript.txt)

---

## 1. Universal Morning Checklist

- [ ] **Arrival Window:** Reach campus between **07:00 AM – 07:30 AM** before security gates congest.
- [ ] **Attire Under Gown:** Wear lightweight, breathable formal clothing (coastal heat and humidity inside tents can be intense).
- [ ] **The Tassel Rule:** Wear your mortarboard (cap) flat. Your tassel hangs on the **RIGHT side** of your face. It is only shifted to the **LEFT side** after the Chancellor officially admits you to your degree.
- [ ] **Seating by School:** Report directly to your faculty's designated seating pavilion. Stay in your department's section so you stand at the proper cue.
- [ ] **Essentials:** Cold water bottle, power bank, pocket mints/tissues.
- [ ] **Meeting Landmark:** Cellular data networks get heavily congested with thousands of attendees. Agree with family on a specific physical meeting landmark (e.g., *Campus Administration Gate* or *Library Steps*) beforehand.

## 2. Full Liturgical Timeline & Exact Spoken Cues

### Stage 1: Academic Procession & Anthems (`[00:01]` – `[11:48]`)
* **`[00:01]`:** Marine Cadets and university brass band march in. Stand up at your seat.
* **`[01:13] - [03:30]`:** Arrival of faculty directors, school deans, University Council, Vice Chancellor Prof. Laila Abubakar, and the Council Chairman / Acting Chancellor.
* **`[04:17]`:** Captain Talib (Director, Maritime Studies) calls the assembly to attention. Stand for the **Kenya National Anthem**, the **East African Community (EAC) Anthem**, and the **TUM Anthem**.

### Stage 2: Invocations & Statutory Constitution (`[11:49]` – `[25:37]`)
* **`[15:08] - [23:59]`:** Interfaith prayers: Sheikh Abdurrahman Said leads the Islamic prayer, followed by Rev. Julius Nudi leading the Christian prayer of thanksgiving.
* **`[25:09]` (Statutory Opening):** VC Prof. Laila Abubakar formally constitutes the assembly:
  > *"Acting on the powers conferred to me by the Universities Act Cap 210 and the Technical University of Mombasa Charter of 2013, I declare this assembly as the 12th congregation of the Technical University of Mombasa. May you all be seated."*  
  *(Sit down).*

### Stage 3: Entertainment & Policy Speeches (`[25:38]` – `[01:46:00]`)
* **`[27:05]`:** TUM Choir performs the classic Zilizopendwa song *Mateso* (originally by Rajab Mzee) and choral spoken-word poetry.
* **`[44:43] - [01:06:48]`:** Recognition of top academic scholars, valedictorian speeches (Terry Charity Wangui Mugo and Marera Nelson Mandela), and partner corporate awards.
* **`[01:06:49]`:** **Vice Chancellor's Welcome Address:** Highlights the launch of Mombasa's first School of Medicine (`[01:18:55]`), BSc in Nautical Science (`[01:19:43]`), Pwani Innovation Week research, and class statistics.
* **`[01:28:24]`:** **Council Chairman / Acting Chancellor Address:** Institutional governance, ethical advice, and charge to graduates.
* **`[01:37:26]`:** **Chief Guest's Keynote Address:** Delivering remarks on behalf of Education Cabinet Secretary Hon. Julius Migos Ogamba and the Principal Secretary for Higher Education.

## 3. The Degree Conferral Sequence (When Your Level is Called)

Degrees are conferred in strict academic order:

### 1. Doctor of Philosophy (PhD) Degrees (`[01:46:00]` – `[02:08:00]`)
* Deans present PhD candidates. Chancellor confers degrees. Candidates are hooded, caps fitted, followed by an official photo session on the dais with university leadership.

### 2. Master's Degrees (`[02:08:00]` – `[02:16:34]`)
* Sequential presentation by Deans of Humanities, Computing, Business, Engineering, and Applied Sciences. Collective admission by the Chancellor.

### 3. Institutional Certificates (`[02:16:35]` – `[02:23:02]`)
* Presentation and formal award of technical and vocational certificates (e.g., Certificate in ICT & Maintenance).

### 4. University Diplomas (`[02:23:03]` – `[02:52:56]`)
* Full diploma roll call across all schools: Maritime, Computing, Applied Sciences, Business, Engineering, and Humanities.

### 5. Bachelor's Degrees by Faculty (`[02:52:57]` – `[03:35:15]`)
Deans present undergraduate cohorts in this exact sequence:
1. **Institute of Computing and Informatics (ICI):** `[02:53:42]` *(BSIT, Computer Science)*
2. **School of Applied and Health Sciences (SAHS):** `[02:57:07]` *(Medical Lab Science, Food Science, Marine Resource Management)*
3. **School of Business (SOB):** `[03:02:35]` *(BCom, BBA, Human Resources, Supply Chain)*
4. **School of Engineering and Technology (SET):** `[03:10:55]` *(Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Building Engineering)*
5. **School of Humanities and Social Sciences (SHSS):** `[03:23:33]` *(Mass Communication, Development Studies)*
6. **Institute of Maritime and Seafaring Studies (IMSS):** `[03:33:04]` *(BSc Nautical Science, Maritime Management)*

* **Standing Cue:** When your Dean calls your program and classification, **stand up** at your seat.
* **Sitting Cue:** Sit down when instructed: *"Graduates, please take your seats until the conferment of the respective degrees."*

---

## 4. The Collective Admission & Tassel Flip (`[03:38:04]` – `[03:40:59]`)

1. **The Call to Rise:**
   > *"Will all the bachelor's degree graduates rise up for the conferment of the respective bachelor's degree by the chancellor!"*
   * All undergraduate candidates across all faculties stand up together.
2. **The Chancellor's Conferment Formula:**
   > *"Acting on the powers conferred to me by the Universities Act Cap 210 and the Technical University of Mombasa Charter of 2013, and having satisfied the University Senate in all subjects for which you were enrolled, trained and examined, I admit the persons presented to me to the respective degrees of Technical University of Mombasa, and by my authority and that of the Senate, give power to read and to do all that appertains to this degree."*
3. **THE MOMENT:**
   * **Move your cap tassel from the RIGHT side to the LEFT side.**
   * Ovations, horn blowing, and cheers—you are officially a university graduate! 🎉

---

## 5. Dissolution & Recession (`[03:41:00]` – `[03:44:13]`)

* **`[03:41:07]`:** Chancellor formally dissolves the assembly: *"I declare the 12th congregation of Technical University of Mombasa dissolved."*
* **`[03:41:54]`:** Stand for the closing **Kenya National Anthem**.
* **`[03:42:56]`:** The announcer delivers the final valediction: *"Congratulations to the 3,281 graduates of the class of 2025. Go forth and serve the world! Let's have the procession."*
* **Procession Leaves First:** Remain standing at your seat until the Chancellor, Council, and faculty procession completely departs before meeting your guests for photos.
